Metric    		     
What it measures    		     
How to read it    
   
First-pass approval rate   		    
Share of AI-generated PRs approved without   change requests   		    
Above 90%: reviews are probably not thorough   enough. Below 30%: improve the prompts or the context given to the model.   
   
Post-merge defect rate   		    
Bugs per PR after merge, for AI-generated   and human-written code   		    
A higher rate for AI-generated code means   the review process misses problems. This is the most reliable metric.   
   
Review time   		    
Time spent reviewing AI-generated PRs   		    
Shorter reviews with a stable defect rate:   the team is improving. Shorter reviews with a rising defect rate: reviewers   are approving without checking.   
   
Change request categories   		    
Change requests tagged by problem type, for   example non-existent API, weak test, or missing edge case   		    
Shows which problems occur most often in   your codebase, so you know where to focus reviews.
